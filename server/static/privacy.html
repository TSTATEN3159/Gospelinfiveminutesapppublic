Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2025

At The Gospel in 5 Minutes, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

When you register or use our app, we may collect: Name, Email address, Phone number (optional), Birth month and day (for personalized content).

Usage Data

We collect information about how you use the app: Daily streak tracking, Bible reading progress, Saved verses and bookmarks, App preferences and settings.

AI Pastor Questions

When you use our AI-powered Biblical guidance feature, your questions are processed by OpenAI's API to provide you with scripture-based responses. These interactions are used solely to deliver the service and are not stored permanently.

Microphone & Voice Search (Optional)

Our app includes an optional voice search feature that allows you to search for Bible verses by speaking. When you use this feature:

  • Microphone Access: The app requests permission to access your device's microphone to convert your speech to text for Bible search
  • Speech Processing: Your voice is processed locally by your device's built-in speech recognition (no audio is sent to our servers or third parties)
  • Opt-In Only: This feature is completely optional - you can always search using the keyboard instead
  • No Audio Storage: We do not record, store, or transmit your voice data. The microphone is only active when you tap the voice search button
  • Browser-Level Privacy: Speech recognition is handled by your web browser's built-in APIs (Web Speech API), following your browser's privacy policies

You can revoke microphone permission at any time through your device or browser settings.

Profile Pictures & Photos (Optional)

Our app allows you to upload a profile picture to personalize your account. When you use this feature:

  • Photo Access: The app requests permission to access your device's camera or photo library to select a profile picture
  • Local Storage Only: Your profile picture is stored locally on your device - it is not uploaded to our servers
  • No Identification Use: We do not use your photo or any likeness to identify you or for facial recognition purposes
  • Not Linked to Identity: Your profile picture is stored separately from any personally identifiable information and cannot be used to track you across other apps or services
  • Opt-In Only: This feature is completely optional - you can use the app without uploading a profile picture
  • Full Control: You can change or remove your profile picture at any time by uploading a new photo or clearing your browser data

Your privacy is important to us. Profile pictures are strictly for personalization within the app and are never shared with third parties, used for advertising, or analyzed for any purpose beyond displaying them in your profile.

Contacts (Optional)

If you choose to use our Friends feature, we request permission to access your contacts to help you find friends who also use the app. Contact data is only used for this purpose and is never sold or shared with third parties.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Personalize daily Bible verses and devotional content
  • Track your spiritual growth progress and streaks
  • Provide AI-powered Biblical guidance through our Ask Pastor feature
  • Send relevant spiritual content and notifications (with your permission)
  • Improve app functionality and user experience
  • Connect you with friends who use the app (with your permission)

3. Data Sharing with Third Parties

OpenAI Services

We use OpenAI's API to power our AI Pastor feature. When you ask a question, it is sent to OpenAI for processing. OpenAI processes this data according to their own privacy policy. You can review OpenAI's privacy practices at https://openai.com/privacy.

No Data Selling

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
  • Secure server infrastructure
  • Limited access to personal information
  • Regular security audits and updates

5. Your Rights and Controls

Data Access

You can access and review your personal information at any time within the app's Support & Privacy section.

Data Export

You have the right to export all your data in JSON format. This feature is available in the Support & Privacy section of the app.

Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time through the app's Support & Privacy section. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Opt-Out

You can opt out of optional features (like contacts access and notifications) at any time through your device's settings or the app's settings page.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app is suitable for all ages (rated 4+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy. You may delete your account at any time, which will permanently remove all your data from our servers.

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the app and updating the "Effective Date" above. Your continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
