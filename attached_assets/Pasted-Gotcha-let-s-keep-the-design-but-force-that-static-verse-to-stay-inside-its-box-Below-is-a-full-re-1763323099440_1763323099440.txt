Key change for the static verse is this block:

<p
  className="
    text-base md:text-xl font-semibold leading-snug mb-3
    max-h-20 md:max-h-24 overflow-hidden break-words
  "
>
  “{featuredVerse.text}”
</p>


text-base md:text-xl → slightly smaller so it fits more naturally.

max-h-20 md:max-h-24 → limits how tall it can get.

overflow-hidden → anything beyond that height is clipped (no running off the card).

break-words → long words wrap instead of pushing outside the box.

3️⃣ Quick environment check

This assumes:
