### Practical notes

* For best rendering across email clients:

  * Keep styles **inline** like we did.
  * Use a **large, non-tiled parchment image** (at least 1200–1600px wide) so the verse area looks smooth.
* If Outlook gives you trouble with background images, you can still fall back to a solid warm color—the parchment will show in most modern clients (Gmail, iOS Mail, Apple Mail, etc.).
