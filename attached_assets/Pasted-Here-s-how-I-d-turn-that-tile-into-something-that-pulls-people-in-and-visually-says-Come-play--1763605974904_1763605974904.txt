Here’s how I’d turn that tile into something that *pulls* people in and visually says:
“Come play, grow, and earn your Bible Expert badge.”

## Concept

**What we add on the tile itself:**

* A **bold headline** and stronger subcopy.
* A **“Daily Streak / Crowns / Title” strip** right on the card so it feels alive, not static.
* A **“New: Daily Challenge” pill** to spark curiosity.
* A **subtle gradient over the photo** so text is super readable.
* A clear **“Earn Bible Expert” hook** under the button.

You can wire this into your stats system later; for now I’ll assume you can pass in props like `streak`, `crowns`, and `title`.
